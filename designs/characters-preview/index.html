Parts, redrawn

A new take on the characters, borrowing from the creatures in Denis’s IFS illustration: bulging staring eyes, the illustration’s red, cobalt, sky blue, mustard and bottle green, with engraved shading and print speckle. Each one still has its own little animation. Tap a card to make it hop.

What changed from the current characters

  • Colours now come from the head illustration instead of pastel pinks and purples.
  • Big white staring eyes with small pupils, like the creatures in the drawing.
  • Engraved hatching for shadows and speckled ink, so they feel printed rather than flat.
  • More creature-like: a little red devil, a frog, a white rabbit, an elephant, a shouting red head.
  • One new part: the little one with the red balloon.
